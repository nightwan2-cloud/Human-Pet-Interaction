The F and P IDs are stable internal labels. Read the matching record before using a reference action; do not paste an ID, a source image description, or this table into the final prompt.

The records describe 143 folder images and 56 PDF image positions. A means visible contact, B means companionship/anticipation or uncertain contact, P means pet-only, N means no visible pet. Use A preferentially for strong interaction; B and P can inform posture or pet appearance but need a newly composed reciprocal event. Near-duplicate images are one action family.

Selection: match the user's exact posture and activity first, then the number and species of pets, then size and contact geometry. If unspecified, rotate among appropriate action families and [pet appearance types](pet-type-library.md). Avoid weighting F003/F007, F093/F094, F096/F101/F102, and P01-12/P01-16 as unrelated ideas. Do not turn an animal-only or mere food-waiting record into a claimed observed happy exchange. A dog's open mouth can be panting or yawning; a cat's relaxed ears, gentle cheek rub, and easy body contact may convey contentment without a dog-like grin. Reconstruct a readable happy, relaxed moment when the user asks for it, with support and expressions appropriate to the species.
